# Your tracks: where new work comes in

Scope: `/tracks` and the upload dialog it shares with the track page's new-version zone. Visitor mode: Operate. Build path: code-led (an extension inside the established world, no image generation). The owner chose the LCD deck from three sketched drop zones on 2026-11-05.

Audience and job: an artist has just bounced a track and wants it up fast, with its details entered once. Most uploads should be a drop, then Enter.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The upload is an empty slot on the player waiting for a file, not a form. It refuses the category default, a dashed "drag and drop" box that could belong to any app, and a multi-step wizard that makes a four-field job feel like a process.

OWN-WORLD: The established world, unchanged. A recessed LCD window with its lip, Silkscreen for the readout, lime only when lit. The dialog is a flat working screen: canvas fill, field-edge outline, graphite fields and buttons, no shadow.

STORY: The artist sees an unlit slot, drags a bounce over it and watches it light, drops it, and finds everything on one screen with the title already filled in. They're told plainly who can hear it. They press Enter, watch the striped bar, and land on the track.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Under the "Your tracks" H1, the deck spans the column: "NO FILE LOADED" and a small line on the left, the primary "Choose a file" on the right, the formats line beneath, then the track list.

FORM: Established-world extension; the deck is this page's one signature, joined by the upload bar only inside the dialog. seed: none (extension inside the established world; no concept roll ran)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction

Holding a file over the deck lights it: the readout turns from unlit to phosphor and says what happens next.

## Unresolved

- Dropping several files as a batch. Only the first goes up for now, and the dialog says so.
